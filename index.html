A Gaming Community Built Around Fun

NEPA TTRPG Club is for people who want to act out their heroic fantasy in a safe and fun environment. We have two groups of gamers: ages 14 to 17, and adults 18+. Whether you are joining with friends, family, or on your own, this club is about learning the game, telling stories, meeting other players, and having a great time together.

Fantasy in Real Life

Step Into a Bigger Story

D&D lets ordinary people become adventurers, heroes, tricksters, spellcasters, and legends. Every game night is a chance to do something brave, funny, clever, or unforgettable.

Be the hero of a shared story.
Big Personalities Welcome

Serious, Silly, Dramatic, or Wild. All Play Styles Welcome.

Some players love roleplay. Some love strategy. Some love combat. Some love jokes, weird plans, and unexpected chaos. Great tables make room for all kinds of fun.

There is no one right way to adventure.
Beginners Belong Here

New Players Do Not Need Experience

If you have never played before, that is completely fine. We can help you understand the basics, learn how turns work, and feel comfortable at the table without needing to know everything ahead of time.

Curiosity is enough. We can help with the rest.
Gaming Community

Make Friends, Find a Table, and Keep Coming Back

The club is not just about learning rules. It is about building a lasting local gaming community, finding people you click with, and creating the kind of game nights people look forward to every week.

A party can start with one survey and grow into a team.

How to Join

We have not scheduled the first club meeting time yet, so the best way to get involved is to complete the interest survey and stay in contact.

1

Fill Out the Survey

Start with one quick sign-up form so we can learn who is interested, what age group you are in, and what kind of games you want to play.

2

Tell Us More About Your Game Style

After you sign up, we will send you a more in-depth survey to learn what kind of game you would like to be part of and other useful details. We use that information to help shape the club itself, pair you into a gaming table of like-minded gamers, and know what to share with you about upcoming meetings, plans, and next steps.

3

Join the Adventure

Once the club schedule is set, you will be ready to jump into games, meet other players, and start building your place in the community.
