What Is a TTRPG?

A TTRPG (Tabletop Role-Playing Game) is a game where players sit together at a table and take on the roles of characters in a shared story.

In this club, we play Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition (2014) with some light homebrew elements to keep things flexible and fun.

Create a Character

Build your own hero, from a powerful wizard to a sneaky rogue.

Play the Game

Roll dice, make decisions, and face challenges as a team.

Tell a Story

Your choices shape the world and the story you experience together.

Our Goal

Our goal is to help players grow their characters to Level 5.

By that point, you will have enough experience to:

  • Join other tables confidently
  • Understand the rules and flow of the game
  • Even start your own game as a Game Master

Who Can Join?

Ages 14–17 and Adults 18+.

No experience needed. New players, returning players, and experienced players are all welcome.
