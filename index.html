What Is a TTRPG?

A TTRPG, or tabletop role-playing game, is a game where players gather around a table and step into the role of characters in a shared story. In this club, we play Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition (2014) with some light homebrew elements to keep things flexible and fun.

What Happens at the Table?

You create a character, work with a group, solve problems, face monsters, make choices, and shape the story together. Some sessions are funny. Some are intense. Some are full of mystery, danger, teamwork, and unexpected heroics.

A Gaming Community Built Around Fun

NEPA TTRPG Club is about more than rules and dice. It is about finding your group, learning the game, sharing stories, and having fun in a safe environment where imagination matters.

Create a Character

Play Someone Brave, Clever, Strange, or Legendary

D&D lets you step into the role of a hero of your own design. You might be a knight, wizard, rogue, cleric, or something entirely your own.

Your imagination becomes part of the world.
Play the Game

Roll Dice, Face Challenges, and Work as a Team

Some moments are tense. Some are hilarious. Some come down to one important choice and one lucky roll. The fun comes from solving problems together and seeing what happens next.

Adventure works best when the whole party joins in.
Tell a Story

Build a Shared Fantasy Story with Real People

Every table creates its own story. You are not just watching fantasy happen. You are shaping it with your decisions, your character, and your group.

No two campaigns ever feel exactly the same.

Our Goal

Our goal is to help players grow their characters to Level 5. By that point, you should have enough experience to understand the flow of the game, join other tables confidently, and even feel prepared to start your own table as a Game Master.

Who Can Join?

We have two groups of gamers: ages 14 to 17, and adults 18+.

New players, returning players, friend groups, families, and experienced players are all welcome.
