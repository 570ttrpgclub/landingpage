What Is a TTRPG?

A TTRPG, or tabletop role-playing game, is a game where players gather around a table and step into the role of characters in a shared story. In this club, you are not just watching fantasy happen. You are helping create it.

What Happens at the Table?

Players create characters, work together in a group, solve problems, face monsters, make choices, and shape the story session by session.

Our Goal

We want players to grow their characters to Level 5 and leave with enough experience to join other tables confidently or even start their own.

Who Can Join?

We currently plan to support three groups: ages 10 to 13, ages 14 to 17, and adults 18+.
